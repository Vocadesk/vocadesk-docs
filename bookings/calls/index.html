    What happens on a booking call¶

    This page follows a booking call from the caller's side. The quoted lines are the fixed words the agent says. Other wording comes from the agent's conversation and varies from call to call. The examples use an appointment with Dana on Tuesday the 12th of November at 2:30pm.

    When the booking steps start¶

    At any point in the call, a caller who wants to look up, check, change, move, reschedule or cancel an appointment they already have is taken into the booking steps. The agent recognises this from what the caller says; there is no keyword to say. It comes before the agent's other triggers about appointments.

    The booking steps aren't offered:

    • on an agent without the bookings switch, or one that isn't in scenario mode;
    • while the agent is forwarding the call or ending it.

    A caller who wants a new appointment, a different practitioner or a different appointment type gets a message taken for the clinic: v1 only moves and cancels existing appointments.

    1. Verifying the caller¶

    The agent asks for the details set in How callers are verified, one at a time:

    Setting The caller is asked for
    Date of birth (default) date of birth
    Full name full name
    Surname and date of birth surname, then date of birth
    Full name and date of birth full name, then date of birth

    If the call has no caller ID (withheld number, web widget, browser test call), the details from Callers with no caller ID are added, surname and date of birth by default. If that setting is Don't book: take a message, the agent says "I can't confirm that on this call, so I'll take a message for the clinic." straight away.

    • Names are captured the same careful way as names in the intake questions, with spelling and a read-back.
    • The date of birth is read back in words, for example "The fourth of March nineteen eighty-five, is that right?". The lookup only runs after a clear yes. A future date, or one more than 120 years ago, is asked for again.
    • The agent says "One moment while I check." while it looks the patient up.

    How the match works. The details must match a Cliniko patient exactly, ignoring capitals, accents, hyphens, apostrophes and spaces (José Smith-Jones matches jose smithjones). There is no guessing at near matches. A full name may use the patient's first name or their preferred first name. When there is a caller ID, it must be one of the phone numbers Cliniko holds for that patient. If it isn't, the unknown-number setting decides: take a message (default), or ask for the full name too.

    If the details don't match, the caller hears that no booking was found with those details, without being told which detail was wrong, and is asked once more for all of them. After a second failure the agent says "I can't confirm that on this call, so I'll take a message for the clinic." and returns to the rest of the call. The booking steps aren't offered again on that call.

    Other limits stop repeated guessing across calls. When one is reached, the caller hears the same "take a message" line:

    Limit Value
    Failed verifications per call 2
    Failed verifications per calling number, per day 6
    Failed number checks against one patient, per day 6 (that patient can't be verified by phone for the rest of the day)
    Failed verifications on calls with no caller ID, per client, per day the daily cap, 10 by default

    Days are counted in UTC. If two patients fit the same details equally well (for example twins calling from a shared number), the agent can't tell them apart and takes a message.

    Once verified, the caller stays verified for the rest of the call. A call can only act on one patient's appointments.

    2. Finding the appointment¶

    The agent reads out up to three upcoming appointments, nearest first, with the practitioner and the time, for example "Tuesday the 12th of November at 2:30pm with Dana". If there are several, the caller can pick by order ("the second one"), day ("the Tuesday one") or practitioner. Only appointments at the businesses chosen for the agent are listed.

    • No upcoming appointment: the agent says it can't find a booking under those details, and offers to take a message, or to transfer the call if the agent has forwarding set up.
    • An appointment that can't be moved by phone (not online-bookable, or longer than its appointment type): the agent offers to cancel it or take a message.
    • An appointment inside the cancellation notice window: it can't be moved or cancelled by phone. The agent takes a message.

    When the agent takes a message it says "I'll pass that on to the clinic." and carries on with the call; the message is in the call's summary.

    3a. Moving an appointment¶

    1. The agent looks up free times for the same practitioner and appointment type at the same business, up to four weeks ahead and within the clinic's online-booking lead time and booking window ("One moment while I check.").
    2. It offers three times at a time, in the clinic's local time. The caller can ask for a day, a date range, or the morning (before 12), afternoon (12 to 5pm) or evening (after 5pm). "Anything later?" gives the next three.
    3. If nothing is free in that range, the agent offers a later window once, then takes a message.
    4. When the caller picks a time, the agent reads the change back: "I'll move your appointment with Dana from Tuesday the 12th of November at 2:30pm to Friday the 15th of November at 10am. Shall I go ahead?"
    5. Only a clear yes makes the change ("yes", "yeah", "go ahead", "that's right", "yes, Friday at 10 is fine"). "Yes, but make it 3", "wait", "I guess so" or a yes followed by "actually…" are not a yes. If the answer is unclear, the agent asks "Shall I go ahead and move it to Friday at 10am?", then "Shall I leave it as it is?". Anything but a clear go-ahead leaves the appointment alone: "No problem, I'll leave your appointment as it is."
    6. After the yes: "One moment while I make that change.", then "Your appointment is now Friday the 15th of November at 10am with Dana."

    If the time was taken meanwhile, the agent says so, looks again once and offers new times. If someone else booked the same time in the moment the change was made, the move is undone: "I'm sorry, that time was just taken by someone else, so your appointment stays as it was, Tuesday the 12th of November at 2:30pm with Dana." The clinic gets an alert email.

    In Cliniko the appointment simply moves, keeping its length. No note is added.

    3b. Cancelling an appointment¶

    1. The agent reads it back: "I'll cancel your appointment with Dana on Tuesday the 12th of November at 2:30pm. Shall I go ahead?"
    2. Only a clear yes cancels it ("yes, cancel it"). Unclear answers get "Shall I go ahead and cancel it?", then "Shall I leave it as it is?".
    3. After the yes: "Your appointment on Tuesday the 12th of November at 2:30pm is cancelled."

    In Cliniko the appointment is cancelled with a cancellation reason the agent picks from what the caller said (feeling better, condition worse, sick, away, work or other) and the note "Cancelled by phone agent at caller's request". Repeats of the appointment are not cancelled.

    4. After the change¶

    What happens next follows After a change:

    • Carry on with the intake questions (default): the agent goes on with the agent's next unanswered question.
    • Close with a booking line: the agent asks whether there is anything else, and if not, ends the call with the booking line before the goodbye.

    If the caller changes their mind after a change, the agent takes a message for the clinic; it doesn't undo the change itself. If Email to the patient is on, the patient gets an email about the change.

    When something goes wrong¶

    Situation The caller hears
    Cliniko is down, slow or busy, or the key stopped working "Sorry, I can't do that right now. I'll pass that on to the clinic."
    The change was sent but Cliniko didn't confirm it in time "I couldn't confirm that went through; the clinic will confirm with you." The clinic gets an alert email to check the appointment.
    Bookings were switched off for the agent or operator, or Cliniko was disconnected, during the call A "take a message" line at the next booking step.

    The agent never retries a change on its own, so a change can't be made twice.

    What the call record shows¶

    • Transcript: every date of birth is replaced with [date of birth], in the caller's and the agent's turns. Appointment times stay.
    • Recording: still holds the spoken date of birth until it is deleted under Keep call data for.
    • Summary and extracted data: never include a date of birth or appointment type. Only the agent's own question fields are kept in the extracted data.
    • Summary email: an Appointment section with one line per outcome. See Running bookings day to day.
    • Audit log: one row per lookup and change. See Running bookings day to day.
